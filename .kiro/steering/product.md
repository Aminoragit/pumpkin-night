# Product Steering — Pumpkin Night

## What this is
Pumpkin Night is a single-page web app that helps one family plan Halloween night.
It covers four jobs, in one place:

1. Plan a trick-or-treat route (stops, minutes per stop, total time, safety notes).
2. Pick costumes that work as a family theme (members, age groups, interests).
3. Keep a small photo album of past Halloweens (captions, years).
4. Pack for the night (checklist) and count down to October 31.

## Who it is for
A family going trick-or-treating together. One person sets the plan up; everyone
uses the same browser to look at it.

## Rules the product follows
- No account, no server, no tracking. All data stays in the browser (localStorage).
- Everything must work offline once the page is loaded.
- Copy is plain and concrete. No marketing language.
- The app must work on a phone and on a desktop.
